The project I'd talk your ear off about

★ FINAL YEAR PROJECT

Can AI tell what's behind the voice?

Phoneme-Informed Speech Impairment Classifier

Speech recognition tools fail people with dysarthria — a motor speech disorder. I dug into why, and found that wav2vec2 (a powerful speech model) barely encodes pitch information.

So I built a Probe-Guided Acoustic Feature Injection framework that feeds the missing pitch and spectral features back into the model at exactly the right layer. The result held up across all 15 test speakers and every severity level.

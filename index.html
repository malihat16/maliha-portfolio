Machine learning, web and mobile

Helping speech recognition understand dysarthric speech

Final year project, 2026. Awarded Best Final Year Project in Software Engineering.

Speech recognition tools fail people with dysarthria, a motor speech disorder. I probed why and found that wav2vec2 barely encodes pitch. So I built a probe-guided feature injection pipeline that feeds pitch and spectral features back into the model at the layer that needs them.

The improvement held for all 15 test speakers and every severity level, and a five-configuration ablation study isolates what each feature contributes.

Python, PyTorch, wav2vec2, librosa, TORGO dataset
